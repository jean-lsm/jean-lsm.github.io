Project Overview

Gameplay and Mechanics

Damnation84 is a fast-paced first-person shooter developed by OutZoom, combining weapon-based movement, combat, and resource management.

  • Weapon-Based Movement: Use the Megiddo hook-gun to pull yourself toward targets and the Arma propulsion gun to push yourself away.
  • Combat: Eliminate enemies efficiently, maintain your momentum, and complete stages before your oxygen runs out.
  • Progression: Earn points through combat and use them to purchase upgrades that change gameplay and expand your abilities.

My Contribution

My professional contribution to Damnation84 involved programming and integrating gameplay and presentation systems.

  • HUD Programming: Programming and integration of the heads-up display and interface systems.
  • Localization Systems: Programming and integration of localization systems for the game's text and interface.
  • Movement Systems: Programming and implementation of player movement systems and related gameplay behavior.
  • Combat Systems: Programming and integration of combat-related gameplay systems and mechanics.
  • Sound Implementation: Implementation and integration of sounds into the game's systems and events.
  • Animation Implementation: Implementation and integration of animations into the game's gameplay systems.
  • Vocal Effects: Programming and integration of vocal effects into the game's systems.

This project represents my professional contribution to a game developed by the OutZoom team.
